SigmaReady – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 November 2026

This policy covers the SigmaReady: Green Belt Prep app for Android (package com.sigmaready.app), published by Midwest Solutions LLC ("we", "us").

Summary

SigmaReady does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The app works fully offline.

Information the app collects

None. The app does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information. It contains no third-party software that collects data, and it makes no network requests.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your study progress – answers, completed lessons, bookmarks, quiz and mock exam history, and settings – in the app's private storage on your device. The app never sends this information to us or to anyone else.

If you have Android's own device backup turned on, Android may include this app's saved progress in your personal Google backup so it can be restored on a new device. That backup is operated by Google under your Google account and is not accessible to us. You can turn it off in your phone's Settings under Backup.

Permissions

The app does not ask for access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files or advertising ID. It declares Android's standard internet permission because the built-in web component that displays its pages expects it, but the app does not use it to send or receive any data.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your progress stays on your device until you remove it. You can delete it at any time with "Reset progress" in the app's Settings, by clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or by uninstalling the app. We hold no copy, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Security

Your progress is kept in the app's private storage, which Android protects from other apps. Nothing is transmitted, so nothing can be intercepted in transit.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking software and shares no data with third parties. The app is sold as a one-time paid download through Google Play. Google handles the payment, your download and your account information under Google's own privacy policy. We do not receive your payment details.

Children

SigmaReady is a professional exam study aid intended for adults. It is not directed at children, and it collects no data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated and the effective date above will change before that version is released.
