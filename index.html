SigmaReady is an independent study guide and practice-test app for the ASQ® Certified Six Sigma Green Belt (CSSGB®) exam. Learn each topic, practise with questions that explain every answer, then sit a full timed mock exam. It is built for quality professionals, engineers and students preparing for certification.

What's inside

  • 24 lessons, six domainsOverview, Define, Measure, Analyze, Improve and Control, with key formulas, exam traps and worked examples.
  • 600 original questionsEvery question explains all four options, so you learn why the wrong answers are wrong.
  • Full mock exam110 questions against a 4.5-hour countdown, with flags, a navigator grid and a score by domain.
  • Calculators that show their workCp and Cpk, Pp and Ppk, DPMO and sigma level, rolled throughput yield and control chart limits, step by step.
  • Decision wizards and tablesWhich test? Which control chart? Plus Z, t and chi-square tables and a searchable formula list.
  • Open-book exam kitA binder tabbing guide and a printable quick-reference formula sheet.
